# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

EventFlex is committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect all members to uphold this code in all project spaces online and offline, including but not limited to pull requests, issue discussions, and other community interactions.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** - Treat everyone with courtesy and respect
- **Being inclusive** - Welcome people regardless of background, experience, or identity
- **Being constructive** - Provide helpful feedback and suggest improvements
- **Being collaborative** - Work together to solve problems and achieve goals
- **Being transparent** - Communicate clearly and honestly

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment** - Insults, derogatory comments, or unwelcome advances
- **Discrimination** - Based on gender, race, ethnicity, age, disability, or any other characteristic
- **Intimidation** - Threats, aggressive behavior, or hostile actions
- **Misinformation** - Deliberately spreading false information
- **Spam** - Excessive or repetitive messages
- **Off-topic behavior** - Derailing discussions from their intended purpose
- **Violating privacy** - Sharing someone's personal information without consent

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. They will:

1. **Document** reported violations with context and evidence
2. **Investigate** complaints fairly and thoroughly
3. **Take action** ranging from warnings to removal based on severity
4. **Appeal** - Provide mechanism for appealing decisions

### Consequences

Depending on severity:

- **First offense:** Warning and explanation
- **Repeated behavior:** Temporary ban (1-7 days)
- **Serious violations:** Permanent removal from community

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Do not engage** - Don't escalate the situation
2. **Report privately** - Email maintainers: [conduct@eventflex.io] (not a real address)
3. **Provide details:**
   - What happened
   - Who was involved
   - When it occurred
   - Any relevant links or screenshots

## Privacy

All reports will be reviewed confidentially. We will:

- Investigate thoroughly
- Protect the reporter's identity
- Take appropriate action
- Inform the reporter of the outcome
